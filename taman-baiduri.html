Overview

Bigger Space for Families Who Want More

Taman Baiduri offers generous semi-detached homes for families ready to upgrade to a bigger, long-term home. With land sizes up to 4,264 sq ft, 4+1 bedrooms and 5 bathrooms, each unit is built for privacy and comfort, on freehold, individual-title land with no shared maintenance obligations.

The development sits within easy reach of Banting town centre, with established roads connecting to KLIA, Jenjarom and Klang — a practical address for families who want space without leaving convenience behind.
