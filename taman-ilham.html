Overview

A Comfortable First Home

Taman Ilham is a close-knit community of 27 terrace homes, completed in 2022 and ready to move in. Each unit offers 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms on a 20 ft x 70 ft freehold, individual-title lot — built for first-time buyers who want space and a manageable monthly instalment, without construction uncertainty.

Set in a peaceful pocket of Banting, residents are close to schools, healthcare and daily necessities, with established roads connecting to Klang and Putrajaya.
